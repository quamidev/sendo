## Shipped [#shipped]

These items were on the V2 plan and are now part of `@quami/sendo` and the registry:

- **Embedded Signup**: `sendo.accounts.completeEmbeddedSignup` and the `sendo-numbers` item ([Embedded Signup](/docs/embedded-signup)).
- **Broadcasts and opt-in**: `sendo.broadcasts`, `sendo.contacts.setOptIn` and the `sendo-broadcasts` item ([Broadcasts](/docs/broadcasts)).
- **Metrics**: SQL views, `sendo.metrics` and the `sendo-metrics` item ([Metrics](/docs/metrics)).
- **Drizzle and Prisma adapters**: `@quami/sendo/adapters/drizzle` and `@quami/sendo/adapters/prisma`, both passing `runAdapterContract` ([Adapters](/docs/adapters)).
- **Voice notes and cost**: the `transcribe` option and per-turn cost ([Voice notes and cost](/docs/voice-notes-and-cost)).
- **Sendo Connect (package side)**: the `connect` option ([Sendo Connect](/docs/sendo-connect)).

### diff CLI [#diff]

`npx @quami/sendo diff` fetches the latest registry items and compares them with your installed files and `sendo_schema_meta.version`, with a changelog. Until then the package warns at startup when the installed schema is older than it expects.

## V3: Quami Cloud transport [#v3]

An optional hosted mode: `transport: quamiCloud({ apiKey })` sends traffic through Quami, which receives webhooks, stores data and delivers the same events. App code stays the same. It needs Meta Tech Provider status, encryption at rest, configurable retention, a DPA and its own spec before it’s built.

- `quamiCloud({ apiKey })` exists as a stub: every method rejects with `NotImplementedError` (`NOT_IMPLEMENTED`).

A broadcast sends one APPROVED WhatsApp template to a list of contacts, a batch per minute, and keeps a result per recipient. Sendo decides who may receive it from the template's category and the contact's opt-in, and you can't override that: the preview tells you how many will get it and why the others are skipped, before anything is sent.

## Who receives a broadcast [#eligibility]

| Template category               | Who receives it                                               | Skipped as  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `MARKETING`                     | Only contacts with `marketingOptIn = true` (an explicit yes). | `no_opt_in` |
| `UTILITY`, `AUTHENTICATION`     | Every contact who hasn't explicitly opted out.                | `opted_out` |
| Any category Sendo doesn't know | Treated as `MARKETING`.                                       | `no_opt_in` |

- **Opted out** means `marketingOptIn = false` **with** a decision date (`optInAt`): the contact said no, by keyword or through your app. A contact nobody asked yet has `optInAt = null`: they get utility messages ("tu cobro de noviembre fue emitido") but not promotions.
- Phones that don't parse are skipped as `invalid_phone` and never stored as contacts.
- Only APPROVED, active templates can be broadcast (`TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND` otherwise). Sync them first with `sendo.templates.sync()`.
- Eligibility is checked again when each message is sent, so a contact who replies BAJA in the middle of a broadcast doesn't get it.

<Callout>

Pick the category honestly when you create the template in Meta. Meta re-categorizes templates whose content doesn't match, and marketing sent as utility can get the template paused.

</Callout>

## Opt-in and opt-out [#opt-in]

Record a contact's explicit decision with `sendo.contacts.setOptIn(contactId, optIn, { source })`. It sets `marketingOptIn` and `optInAt`, and emits `contact.opt_in` (repeating the stored decision is a no-op).

<Sample file="broadcasts.ts" region="opt-in" title="ts" />

**Opt-out keywords.** An inbound message that is exactly one of the keywords (default `BAJA`, `STOP`, `ALTO`, `CANCELAR`), after trimming and ignoring case and accents (`" Cancelár "` matches), opts the contact out: `marketingOptIn = false`, `contact.opt_in` with `source: "keyword"`, a confirmation message inside the 24-hour window (`optOut.confirmation`, default `BUILT_IN_MESSAGES[locale].optOutConfirmation`, so English unless `createSendo({ locale: "es" })`), and no agent turn for that message. "Quiero darme de baja" doesn't match; handle phrases in your agent or a rule if you want them.

Add the opt-out sentence to your marketing templates ("Responde BAJA si no quieres recibir promociones") so contacts know how.

## Preview, create and follow [#api]

Every `sendo.broadcasts` method takes an admin actor (`broadcasts.manage`). Recipients are contact ids, `{ phone }` (upserted as a contact on create) or either with its own `variables`, which win over the shared ones.

- `create` stores the broadcast as `queued` and each recipient as `pending` or `skipped` with the reason. Nothing is sent yet.
- **The tick sends it.** `sendo.maintenance.tick()` (every minute, see [the tick](/docs/workflows#tick)) sends up to `ratePerMinute` pending recipients per broadcast (default 60), using the same conversation as any other message. A broadcast never moves a conversation to `human` and never runs the agent for the template; replies land in the normal conversation and the inbox.
- Status webhooks move each recipient to `sent`, `delivered`, `read` or `failed` (with Meta's error). The broadcast ends `done` when nothing is pending.
- `cancel(id)` turns the pending recipients into `cancelled`. Messages already sent stay sent.

## Billing pauses and limits [#billing]

Meta bills every delivered message directly to the WhatsApp Business account that owns the number, by category and the recipient's country. Sendo moves no money and never copies Meta's rates: see [Meta's official pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing).

- **No payment method (Meta 131042):** the broadcast pauses with `pauseReason: "billing"` and the number's `billingReady` becomes `false`. Pending recipients stay pending. Once the business adds a payment method and Meta confirms it (the tick re-checks billing hourly, or call `sendo.accounts.refreshBilling(id)`), the next tick resumes the broadcast by itself.
- **Rate or tier limits (130429 and the other rate-limit codes):** that minute's pass stops and the recipient stays pending; the next tick continues. Meta also caps how many unique contacts a number can reach per day by its messaging tier.
- **Template no longer approved:** the broadcast stops as `failed` and its pending recipients are cancelled.

## The Envíos screen (`broadcasts`) [#registry]

```shell
npx shadcn add @sendo/broadcasts
```

The "Envíos" screen at `/inbox/broadcasts` (admins only):

1. **Nuevo envío:** pick a number, an approved template (with its preview and the category rule), its variables, one of your app's contact lists or filters, and the rate.
2. **Revisar envío:** total, eligible and skipped counts by reason, the estimated time, and the cost note linking to Meta's pricing. "Enviar a N contactos" is disabled when nobody is eligible.
3. **Progress:** counts per state (refreshed every 5 seconds while sending), per-recipient results with Meta's error on failed rows, "Cancelar envío", and a notice with a link to add a payment method when the broadcast is paused for billing.

Before using it, edit `app/(protected)/inbox/broadcasts/broadcasts-config.ts`: implement `requireUser()` (it throws until you do) and return your audiences from `broadcastAudiences`. Recipients are always resolved on the server from the audience id; the browser never sends a phone list. The screen also needs the tick route from `workflows`. Details in the item's `AGENTS.md`.

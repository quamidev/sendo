`sendo.snippets` and `sendo.tags` (schema v2). Snippets: `list({ actor, accountId? })` (team snippets plus the actor's personal ones, optionally only those usable on a number), `get`, `upsert({ id?, shortcut, body, visibility?, accountId? }, { actor })`, `delete` and `render(snippetId, { actor, conversationId })` → `{ text, unresolved }`, which fills the built-ins `{{nombre}}`, `{{telefono}}`, `{{agente}}` and the values of the new `resolveSnippetVariables({ contact, conversation, account })` config hook, leaving unresolved variables as written. Team snippets need `snippets.manage_tenant`; personal ones are visible and editable only by their owner; shortcuts are unique per tenant and owner (`ConstraintViolationError`). Tags: `list({ actor })` for every role, and `upsert({ id?, name, color }, { actor })`, `delete` (also removes the tag from every conversation) and `usage` with `tags.manage`; colors come from `TAG_COLORS`. Browser-safe helpers `searchSnippets` (accent- and case-insensitive), `findSnippetVariables`, `fillSnippetVariables`, `normalizeSearchText` and `SNIPPET_SHORTCUT`. The `sendo-inbox` registry item adds the composer with Responder / Nota interna, the `/` snippet picker with unresolved-variable highlighting, the tag picker, the assign menu and the snippets / tags managers; re-copy `actions.ts`, `page.tsx`, `inbox.tsx`, `composer.tsx` and the new component files.

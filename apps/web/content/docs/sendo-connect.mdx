Sendo is free and MIT licensed. The only paid piece is **Sendo Connect**: Quami acts as the Meta **Tech Provider** for your app, so you can offer “Connect your WhatsApp” without going through Meta’s business verification and app review yourself. Price: **coming soon** (per connected number, per month; see [Pricing](/pricing)).

<Callout variant="warning" title="The service is opening">

The package side below ships in `@quami/sendo`. Connect accounts (API key, signing secret, panel) open when Quami’s Tech Provider verification is approved; until then, use [your own Meta app](/docs/meta-setup).

</Callout>

## What changes with Connect [#what-changes]

- **Configuration only.** The package, the migrations and the registry components are identical; you add `connect: { apiKey, signingSecret }` to `createSendo`.
- **Signup** goes through Quami’s Meta app: the number’s token is returned to your server once and never stored by Connect.
- **Webhooks** arrive through Quami’s relay, signed with your own secret (`X-Sendo-Signature`), and are verified fail-closed by your app.
- **Sending** still goes directly from your server to Meta with the customer’s token.

## Setup [#setup]

1. In the Connect panel, create a project and copy the **API key** and the **signing secret** into `SENDO_CONNECT_API_KEY` and `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET` (`.env.local` and your deployment).
2. Register your webhook URL in the panel: `https://<your-domain>/api/sendo/webhook` (the `webhook` route). You don’t configure a webhook in Meta.
3. In `lib/sendo.ts`, remove `appSecret` and add `connect`. Set `meta.appId` / `configId` (and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENDO_META_APP_ID` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENDO_META_CONFIG_ID` for the connect button) to Quami’s values from the panel.
4. Run `npx @quami/sendo doctor --url https://<your-domain>/api/sendo/webhook` ([checks below](#doctor)).

## Webhook signature [#signature]

With `connect` set, `sendo.handleWebhook` (and `toNextHandlers`) accepts a `POST` only when `X-Sendo-Signature` is valid:

```
X-Sendo-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex hmac_sha256(signingSecret, t + "." + rawBody)>[,v1=<hex>]
```

- The timestamp must be within **5 minutes** of your server’s clock (either direction), so a captured request can’t be replayed later. Keep the server clock synced.
- The comparison is constant-time. Several `v1=` values are allowed (any may match) so the secret can be rotated ([rotation](#rotation)).
- Anything else (missing, malformed, stale, wrong secret, body changed) is `401` and nothing is stored.
- **Meta’s `X-Hub-Signature-256` is not accepted** while `connect` is set, even if `appSecret` is still configured. Without `connect`, nothing changes: Meta’s signature is the only one checked.

### Rotating the signing secret [#rotation]

A project can have **two active signing secrets**. While both are active, the relay sends one `v1=` per secret, and your app accepts the request when any of them matches its `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET`. To rotate without dropping a webhook:

1. In the panel, add a second signing secret. Deliveries now carry two `v1=` values; your app keeps verifying with the old secret.
2. Set `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET` to the new secret in `.env.local` and your deployment, and redeploy.
3. Check that deliveries still succeed (`sendo doctor`, or [Send test event](#test-event)), then revoke the old secret in the panel.

The panel refuses a third active secret.

## Test event [#test-event]

The panel’s **Send test event** button signs a synthetic body with your project’s active secrets and sends it to your webhook URL:

```
POST https://<your-domain>/api/sendo/webhook
X-Sendo-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>[,v1=<hex>]

{"object":"sendo_test"}
```

With `connect` set, `sendo.handleWebhook` checks the signature exactly as for a real webhook. A valid test event gets `200` with `Cache-Control: no-store`, and nothing is stored, processed or emitted. A missing or wrong signature is `401`, so a failed test points at `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET` or the server clock. The body’s `object` is exported as `CONNECT_TEST_EVENT_OBJECT`. Without `connect`, the body gets no special handling.

## Signup through Connect [#signup]

`sendo.accounts.completeEmbeddedSignup(input)` takes the same input and returns the same `Account`. With `connect`, steps 1–4 of the [direct sequence](/docs/embedded-signup#sequence) are one call to Connect:

```
POST {baseUrl}/v1/signup/complete
Authorization: Bearer <apiKey>
{ "code", "wabaId", "phoneNumberId", "coexistence", "pin" }   →   200 { "token", "displayPhone" }
```

`pin` is the admin’s PIN or a random 6 digits (`null` for coexistence numbers, which skip `register`). Then Sendo continues exactly as in the direct path: `storeToken`, insert or reconnect the account (`onboardedVia: "connect"`), first number becomes the default, billing check. A failure after `storeToken` compensates the same way (no row left, `deleteToken`, app unsubscribed).

Errors keep the same codes. Connect reports Meta failures as `{ "error": { "code", "message", "step", "metaCode", "subcode", "fbtraceId" } }`, which map to `SIGNUP_CODE_EXPIRED`, `META_PERMISSION_MISSING`, `PIN_CONFLICT` or `SIGNUP_FAILED` with that `step`. A rejected API key (401) is `CONFIG_ERROR` naming `SENDO_CONNECT_API_KEY`; Connect unreachable or an answer without a token is `SIGNUP_FAILED` with `step: "connect"`. `meta.appId` and `appSecret` are not needed on your side.

## Disconnecting a number [#disconnect]

`sendo.accounts.disconnect(id)` (and `sendo accounts disconnect <id>`) on a number with `onboardedVia: "connect"` also tells Connect, so it stops relaying and billing that number:

```
POST {baseUrl}/v1/numbers/{phoneNumberId}/disconnect
Authorization: Bearer <apiKey>
→ 200 { "ok": true }   ·   404 NUMBER_NOT_FOUND   ·   403 PROJECT_SUSPENDED
```

It’s best effort: if Connect fails or can’t be reached (30-second timeout), Sendo logs a warning without the key and the number is still disconnected in your app. Disconnect it in the panel if the warning appears. The CLI notifies Connect when `SENDO_CONNECT_API_KEY` (and `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET`) are set.

## Suspension [#suspension]

When a payment fails, Connect gives the account a **7-day grace period** (with warnings by email and in the panel). After it, the project is **suspended**:

- The relay keeps queuing your webhooks for their 24-hour window, but doesn’t deliver them.
- Every `/v1/*` call answers `403 { "error": { "code": "PROJECT_SUSPENDED", … } }`: signup fails and disconnect notifications are only logged.
- `sendo doctor` reports `connect:key` as “Connect project suspended — update payment at app.sendo.quami.dev”, with a `billing` fix.

Paying un-suspends the project immediately, and anything still inside its 24-hour window is delivered.

## Errors from Connect [#errors]

Every 4xx and 5xx answer from Connect has the same envelope, parsed into `ConnectRequestError` (`status`, `errorCode`, `step`, `metaCode`, `subcode`, `fbtraceId`):

```
{ "error": { "code", "message", "step"?, "metaCode"?, "subcode"?, "fbtraceId"? } }
```

The codes are exported as `CONNECT_ERROR_CODES`: `UNAUTHORIZED` (401, missing or invalid key), `PROJECT_SUSPENDED` (403), `NUMBER_NOT_FOUND` (404), `NUMBER_IN_USE` (409, the number is active in another project), `EARLY_ACCESS` (signup before Meta approval), `INVALID_REQUEST` (400), `META_ERROR` (a signup step failed on Meta) and `INTERNAL_ERROR` (5xx). `createConnectClient({ config: { apiKey, baseUrl } })` returns the same client the package uses (`completeSignup`, `getWebhook`, `disconnectNumber`).

## Doctor checks [#doctor]

When `SENDO_CONNECT_API_KEY` or `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET` is set, `sendo doctor` requires both (and no longer requires `SENDO_META_APP_SECRET`) and calls `GET {baseUrl}/v1/projects/current/webhook` (`SENDO_CONNECT_BASE_URL` overrides the base URL):

| Check              | Fails when                                                                                                                         | Fix                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `connect:key`      | Connect rejects the key, or can’t be reached; “Connect project suspended” on `403 PROJECT_SUSPENDED`                               | Copy the current key from the panel; check `SENDO_CONNECT_BASE_URL`; when suspended, update payment at app.sendo.quami.dev |
| `connect:webhook`  | No URL registered, or a different one from `--url`                                                                                 | Register `https://<your-domain>/api/sendo/webhook` in the panel                                                            |
| `connect:delivery` | The relay’s last delivery got a non-2xx answer (401 = signing secret mismatch or clock skew); warns when nothing was delivered yet | Fix `SENDO_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET` or the route; the relay retries for 24 h                                                |

## What stays the same [#same]

- Meta bills message costs to your customer’s WhatsApp Business account, not to Quami ([Meta pricing](/docs/meta-pricing)). Connect is billed per connected number.
- Before charging, Connect shows the billing caveat: “Meta bills messages directly to your customer; they need a card on their account.” Until the customer adds a card, sends fail with 131042 and the number shows “payment method missing” ([billing](/docs/embedded-signup#billing)).
- Your data stays in your database.

## Privacy [#privacy]

With Connect, every webhook for your customers’ numbers passes through Quami’s relay before it reaches your app. What Connect sees and keeps:

- A webhook body is **held encrypted only while its delivery is pending**, and deleted on success or when the 24-hour retry window ends.
- Bodies are **never logged**.
- Only delivery metadata (timestamp, project, number, status code, attempt count, latency) is kept, for **7 days**.
- **Quami’s Meta app** is the one connected to your customers’ WhatsApp accounts. The number’s access token is returned to your server once and never stored by Connect.

The [pricing page](/pricing) states exactly this. Apps with sensitive data (health, for example) should use **their own Meta app** instead, so no third party sits in the message path.

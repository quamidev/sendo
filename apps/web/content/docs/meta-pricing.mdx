Sendo moves no money. **Meta bills the owner of the WhatsApp Business account** (the building, the clinic, your customer) directly, per delivered message. Sendo and Quami never re-bill messages. This page explains what a price depends on and links to Meta’s official rate card; it never copies Meta’s rates, which changed twice in fifteen months and change again by country, category and date.

## Official sources [#sources]

- **[Meta’s official rate card and pricing rules](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)**: how messages are billed, the categories, free entry points, announced changes and the rate cards per market. This is the only source for numbers.
- [Meta Business Suite billing](https://business.facebook.com/billing_hub): where the account owner adds a payment method and sees invoices.

## What a price depends on [#dimensions]

Meta bills **per delivered message**. The price of one message depends on:

- **Category.** Every template has one: **marketing**, **utility** or **authentication**. Replies inside the 24-hour customer service window are **service** messages. Pick the category honestly when you create a template; Meta re-categorizes templates whose content doesn’t match ([broadcasts](/docs/broadcasts#eligibility)).
- **The recipient’s country code.** The rate depends on where the number you send to is registered, not where your business is. Meta groups many countries into regional markets: **Guatemala falls under “Rest of Latin America”**, so look up that row on the rate card, not a Guatemala row.
- **The date.** Meta publishes changes ahead of time on the page above; a rate card is valid for a period, not forever.

Instagram and Messenger messages are not billed per message.

## Service messages are changing [#service-messages]

Meta has announced changes to how **service messages** (replies inside the 24-hour window) are billed, and providers describe the new rule differently. Sendo doesn’t claim either version: read the current rule on [Meta’s pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) and check your own invoices in [Meta Business Suite billing](https://business.facebook.com/billing_hub).

## A payment method on every account [#billing-ready]

The account owner must add a payment method in Meta. Until they do, sends fail with Meta error 131042. Sendo tracks this as `billingReady` on each number: the numbers screen shows “falta método de pago”, sends throw with the `billing_not_ready` hint, `sendo doctor` warns, and broadcasts pause until it is fixed. See [Embedded Signup](/docs/embedded-signup#billing).

## Estimating your costs [#estimate]

Count the messages you send per month by category and by the recipient’s market, multiply by the rates on [Meta’s current rate card](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing), and add service messages under the rule Meta applies at the time. [Metrics](/docs/metrics) gives you message counts per day to check the estimate against Meta’s invoices.
